Privacy Policy for Jitta Drives & Jitta Partner

Last Updated: October 17, 2026 | Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Welcome to Jitta Drives (operated by Xiadot, hereafter referred to as "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies to our passenger application (Jitta Drives - User, com.xiadot.jittadrives.user) and driver partner application (Jitta Drives - Driver, com.xiadot.jittadrives.driver).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, how it is shared, and your rights regarding your data in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on whether you use our services as a Passenger or as a Driver Partner, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Details: Your name, mobile phone number, and email address collected during registration or login.
  • Authentication Information: One-Time Passwords (OTPs) and verification tokens sent via SMS to verify your mobile identity securely.
  • Trip & Booking Information: Pickup location, drop-off destination, trip distance, duration, fare details, and trip completion history.
  • Payment Details: Transaction records, mode of payment (UPI, Cash, Wallet), and payment status (we do not store raw card credentials or bank PINs).
  • Driver Verification Documents (Driver App Only): Driving License (DL), Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), Vehicle Insurance, Vehicle Permit, and Driver profile photos uploaded for statutory verification and ride safety.

2. Location Information (Crucial for Taxi Services)

Notice on Location Access: Precise location data is essential for the core functioning of Jitta Drives to match riders with nearby drivers, navigate routes, and ensure passenger safety.

  • For Passengers (User App): We collect precise and approximate location data when the app is running in the foreground to detect your current pickup point, show nearby available taxis, compute accurate fares, and calculate real-time estimated time of arrival (ETA).
  • For Drivers (Driver App): We collect precise location data while the driver is Online in both foreground and background (when the app is open or minimized) to:
    • Receive incoming ride requests nearest to your location.
    • Provide turn-by-turn navigation to pickup and drop locations.
    • Enable live vehicle tracking on the passenger's map during active trips.
    • Calculate exact trip distance and fare accurately.
    Drivers can stop background location tracking at any time by toggling the app status to "Offline".

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for legitimate operational and safety purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing your user and driver accounts.
  • Connecting riders with the nearest available drivers efficiently.
  • Facilitating secure in-app payments, fare settlements, and driver payouts.
  • Providing live trip tracking and 4-digit ride start OTP verification to prevent unauthorized rides.
  • Emergency safety assistance, SOS support, and customer dispute resolution.
  • Preventing fraud, unauthorized bookings, and ensuring driver identity compliance.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may share necessary data with trusted third-party providers strictly to facilitate app services:

  • Mapping & Geocoding: Google Maps Platform APIs for place autocomplete, geocoding, and route calculation.
  • Authentication & Notifications: Google Firebase / SMS Gateway providers to send secure login OTPs and trip notification alerts.
  • Payment Processors: Authorized Indian payment aggregators and UPI providers for secure in-app transactions.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards (including SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption) to protect your personal and location data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Account & Data Deletion Rights

In compliance with Google Play requirements, you have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

To delete your Jitta Drives account and associated data:

  • Go to Settings / Profile in the app and select "Delete Account", or
  • Email our data protection team at Jittadrives@gmail.com or contact our helpline at +91 82204 44321 with your registered mobile number.

Upon verification, your account and associated records will be permanently deleted or anonymized within 30 days, subject to legal and regulatory accounting record-keeping requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
