Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Jitta Drives ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Jitta Drives passenger ride-hailing application and the Jitta Captain partner driver application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information collected through our mobile applications and websites.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, and profile photo.
  • Location Telemetry: Precise real-time GPS location collected during ride booking, active trips, and driver navigation for route calculation, fare estimation, and safety tracking.
  • Captain Partner Verification: Driving license credentials, vehicle registration certificates, vehicle insurance, and background verification records.
  • Transaction Records: Trip receipts, distance, duration, fare breakdown, payment method (cash/UPI), and date-time logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To match passengers with nearby verified captains and calculate optimal driving routes.
  • To provide live ride-sharing and safety features, including the 4-digit start OTP and SOS alerts.
  • To process earnings, disburse driver payouts, and generate electronic GST receipts.
  • To detect fraudulent activities, ensure vehicle safety compliance, and investigate disputes.

4. Location Data Practices

Location data is captured only when the application is active or during an ongoing ride. Driver partners transmit location in the foreground while online to receive ride dispatches. We do not sell or monetize personal location records to third-party advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain user profile and trip logs for legitimate operational, accounting, and legal requirements. Users can request complete account and data removal anytime at our Account Deletion Page.
