Account & Data Deletion Request

For Jitta Drives (Passenger) and Jitta Partner (Driver)

At Jitta Drives, we value your privacy and empower you to have full control over your personal data. In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and data protection standards, users and driver partners can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. Once completed, your profile, active bookings, ride history, wallet balance, and saved locations cannot be recovered.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account using any of the following 3 options:

  1. Option 1: Inside the Mobile App
    Open the Jitta Drives app → Go to Menu / Profile → Select Settings → Tap "Delete My Account" → Confirm with your OTP.
  2. Option 2: Direct Helpline or WhatsApp
    Call or send a WhatsApp message to our support helpline at +91 82204 44321 with your registered mobile number and reason for deletion.
  3. Option 3: Email Request
    Send an email from your registered email ID to Jittadrives@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request - [Your Mobile Number]".

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon processing your deletion request, the following information is permanently erased from our active databases:

  • Your personal profile (Full name, phone number, email address, profile picture).
  • Authentication credentials and saved device tokens.
  • Saved home, work, and frequent location addresses.
  • Driver documents (Driver License, RC, Insurance photos) for Driver accounts.
  • Live GPS tracking data and trip history.

Data Retention Policy (Legal & Tax Obligations)

Certain financial transaction and invoice records related to completed rides may be retained in encrypted archives for up to 180 days solely to comply with applicable statutory accounting, tax, fraud prevention, and regulatory requirements under Indian law, after which they are completely purged.
